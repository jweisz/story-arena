Write in Neal Stephenson's style: Employ a maximalist approach with extensive, meticulous detail, especially for technology and scientific concepts. Integrate frequent 'infodumps' of highly researched technical, historical, or philosophical information directly into the narrative. Craft elaborate plots that interweave numerous technological and sociological ideas. Develop realistic, flawed characters with complex motivations, avoiding simplistic good vs. evil portrayals. Maintain a satirical, intellectually curious, and often humorous tone. Allow characters to take advanced technologies for granted in their dialogue. Ground the narrative in realistic extrapolations of current trends.
